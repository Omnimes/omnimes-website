In 2026 [Multiprojekt Automatyka](https://multiprojekt.pl) — the maker of the **OmniMES** system — entered a strategic partnership with [Relpol S.A.](https://relpol.com.pl), a Polish manufacturer of relays and network meters. The joint offering combines two layers needed to deploy energy management under ISO 50001: **certified Relpol RMM energy meters** and the **OmniMES system with the OmniEnergy module**.

The result for the customer: one hardware vendor, one software vendor, one invoice, one point of technical support — instead of three separate integration projects. The RMM meters (three-phase energy analyzers with Modbus RTU as well as TCP/IP communication) share a jointly tested configuration with OmniMES, and new meter firmware releases are validated on the software side before publication.

## Scope of the joint offering

From a single conversation with Multiprojekt Automatyka the end customer receives a complete set: certified Relpol RMM meters sized to the number of measurement points, an industrial communication gateway with pre-configured register mapping, and the OmniMES system with the OmniEnergy module — a ready machine park structure, pre-defined energy performance indicators (EnPI) and report templates for the ISO 50001 management review.

The whole chain — from the DIN-rail measurement, through the telemetry stream, to the management dashboard — is designed and tested together. For plants that already have Relpol RMM meters installed, rolling out OmniMES boils down to connecting the gateway and configuring the park structure, **without replacing the existing measurement infrastructure**.

## Goal and benefits

The combined metering-and-software set addresses concrete needs of a mid-sized Polish production plant:

- **ISO 50001 review evidence** — an audit trail of measurements, a record of manually entered values, periodic reports generated from a single data source
- **Energy visibility at the level of a single area** — not just aggregate consumption from the main meter, but a breakdown by lines and energy-intensive processes
- **Early detection of losses** — consumption summaries split into working and non-working hours, indicating hidden operating cost
- **Shorter supply chain** — Polish hardware, Polish software, Polish-language technical support, without dependence on foreign vendors

The full description of the partnership and the joint offering is available in the article [OmniMES × Relpol partnership — a smart energy metering network](/en/blog/omnimes-relpol-partnership-smart-energy-metering-network).
