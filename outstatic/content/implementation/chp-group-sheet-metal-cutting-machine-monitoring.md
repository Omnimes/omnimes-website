**CHP Group**, based in Bytom, Poland, supplies complete solutions for flow, temperature and pressure sensors as well as high-pressure manifolds. The company also offers sheet metal cutting using three methods: oxy-fuel (gas), plasma and laser.

## The challenge

Cutting machines consume a lot of electricity and compressed air. Without metering at the level of each machine, it is hard to tell how much each of them costs to run and how efficiently it is used.

## Scope of the implementation

At CHP Group we implemented **OmniMES** with the **OmniEnergy** module. The system monitors all three cutting machines and records in real time:

- the operating status of each machine, i.e. run time and downtime,
- electricity consumption,
- compressed air consumption.

## Results

CHP Group now has full visibility of its cutting costs: it sees the actual run time and downtime of each machine and the utility cost of each cutting method. This allows the company to:

- **price its services more accurately** and protect its margin,
- **spot losses quickly**, such as energy drawn during downtime or leaks in the compressed air system,
- **plan machine loading and fleet expansion** based on data rather than estimates.
